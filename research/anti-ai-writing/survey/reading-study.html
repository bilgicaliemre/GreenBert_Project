Which one reads better?

A short reading study for a high-school research project on writing. You will read 6 pairs of short texts written for the same assignment and answer two questions about each pair. It takes about 20 minutes.

Before you start

  • All texts were produced with AI writing tools for this study, in different ways. The students and people named in them are fictional.
  • We do not collect your name or email. We ask your role and whether English is your first language.
  • Your answers are saved as a short code that you send to the researcher. You can stop at any time; nothing is recorded until you send the code.
  • If you are under 18, please take part only with a parent's or guardian's permission.
