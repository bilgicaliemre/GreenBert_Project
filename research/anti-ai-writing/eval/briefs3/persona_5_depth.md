## Part 1: brainstorm

Student: Kacper Grabowski, 17, last year of liceum (4th class, math-physics profile), Gdańsk, Poland.

Q: What do you do on a normal Saturday?
A: Sleep till ten, then the bike room. We live in the falowiec in Przymorze, the wave building, it's like 850 meters
long, tourists come and take photos of it, which is weird, it's just where I live. In the basement of our staircase
there's the rowerownia, the bike room, and since two years one corner is mine. Two shelves, a folding table from

tram with a broken scooter on my shoulder and she saw me in the mirror and just, nothing, no face. At dinner she said

Q: What's something you're weirdly good at?

## Part 2: depth questions (second session)

Q: Walk me through what exactly happened with Oleh's scooter.
A: One group of ten was at 2.9 volts when the rest were almost 4. I replaced the cells with ones I took out of a pack
from a scooter I bought in Chełm, the district, not the city. I tested them on my Liitokala charger, two came out
lower capacity but I thought, it's fine, it's a mix. I soldered the strips because I didn't have a spot welder,
those are 400 złoty. Then day nineteen, 22:10, Grunwaldzka near Galeria Bałtycka, raining, it cut out. He still had
an order in the bag. He came down to the bike room almost at midnight, wet.

Q: What were you thinking when he walked in?
A: The 380. Honestly. Like, calculating. I said "it got wet" before I unscrewed anything. Then I opened it while he
watched and there's a bit of water in the bottom, there always is, it's Gdańsk. So I pointed at it. I gave him 150
and said that's fair because of the water. He said "dobra", okay, and then on the phone in Ukrainian walking out, to
someone. I don't know what he said.

Q: When did you know it was the repair?
A: Two days after I opened the pack properly. One of my solder joints was cracked, and the cell next to it, I'd
cooked it. And the thing is, in winter on the Facebook group I argued for like three days that you can solder cells
if you're fast enough. I was on that side. I deleted the comments in April.

A: Spot welder from AliExpress, 280 złoty, a month later. A fire extinguisher, 89 at Castorama, it hangs by the
socket. And I don't do batteries for other people anymore. Sounds like a lesson, I know, but no, I just stopped
doing the part where I can mess up. Batteries. Which is, yeah, the thing I told you I want to study. So I don't
know what that is. Also I still leave packs
charging overnight sometimes. In a metal ammo box. Don't write that.

A: That he needed it for work. He told me when he brought it, "I need it for work, it must be good." I used

anyway. Both, same time, yeah.

A: The 230. His number is still in my phone as "Oleh Pro2 Glovo". I've opened BLIK like four times. I have 230, that's
not the problem. The problem is there's the message box and I'd have to write something in it, and what do I write. I
saw him in June on Grunwaldzka on a new Ninebot, not from me. I didn't wave or anything. He never left a review on
