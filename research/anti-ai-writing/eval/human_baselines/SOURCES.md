# Human baseline texts (scores in ../results/)
- Hamilton College "Essays That Worked": Class of 2022 (https://www.hamilton.edu/admission/apply/college-essays-that-worked/2022-essays-that-worked) and Class of 2018 (https://www.hamilton.edu/admission/apply/college-essays-that-worked/2014-essays-that-worked): 14 essays, split by topic.
- Connecticut College "Essays That Worked", two essays from the Class of 2025 (https://www.conncoll.edu/admission/apply/essays-that-worked/). Score files call them conn2025_a and conn2025_b; we do not pair writers' names with detector scores.
- PERSUADE 2.0 corpus (Crossley et al. 2024, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0): 6 "Cell phones at school" (grade 8, score 5-6), 6 "Phones and driving" (score 6), 6 "Distance learning" (grade 11, score 6), all with at least 300 words; selection script logic in DEVLOG section 2.2.
- Science explainers (2020): Bert Hubert, "Reverse Engineering the source code of the BioNTech/Pfizer SARS-CoV-2 Vaccine"; The Conversation, "How mRNA vaccines from Pfizer and Moderna work..." (two excerpts).
Texts are not redistributed here; re-fetch from the sources.
