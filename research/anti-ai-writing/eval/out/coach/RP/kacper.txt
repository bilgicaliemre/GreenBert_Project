Seven minutes and forty seconds. This is how long it takes me to change an inner tube on a Xiaomi M365, wheel off to wheel on. My friend Szymon timed it. Then he timed a guy on YouTube: nineteen minutes.

I live in Gdańsk, in the falowiec, a block of flats 850 meters long. In our basement is a bike room, and for two years one corner has been mine: two shelves, a folding table from Lidl and one socket by the door. This is where I run KacperFix. I buy broken electric scooters on OLX (Polish Craigslist), repair them and sell them. Since summer 2024 I have fixed 41 scooters and have 4.9 stars from 37 reviews.

But the problem I want to write about is not a flat tire. It is a battery, and the problem was me.

In March a Glovo courier named Oleh brought me his Pro 2. The battery was dying on every hill. He told me, "I need it for work, it must be good." One group of ten cells was at 2.9 volts while the rest were almost 4. I replaced them with used cells from another scooter, because new ones would eat my margin. I tested them and two had lower capacity, but I thought, "it's fine, it's a mix." I knew he needed it for work, and I put the used cells in anyway. I had no spot welder, so I soldered the strips. Oleh paid 380 złoty and I gave him a handwritten card: "gwarancja 3 miesiące", three months warranty.

Nineteen days later, at 22:10, the scooter cut out on Grunwaldzka. It was raining and he still had an order in his bag. He pushed it four kilometers and came down to the bike room almost at midnight, wet.

I would like to say my first thought was about him. It was about the 380 złoty. I said "it got wet" before I even unscrewed anything. When I opened it there was a little water at the bottom, there always is, it's Gdańsk, so I pointed at it. I gave him back 150 and said that was fair because of the water. He said "dobra", okay, and walked out talking on the phone in Ukrainian.

Two days later I opened the pack properly. One of my solder joints was cracked and the cell next to it was cooked, by me. In winter I had argued for three days on a Facebook group that you can solder cells if you are fast enough. I was on that side. In April I deleted my comments.

A month later I bought a spot welder for 280 złoty and a fire extinguisher, which now hangs next to the socket. And I stopped doing batteries for other people. This is strange, because batteries and controllers are the parts I find most interesting, and also what I am worst at. Mechanically you can understand a scooter in one hour. The electrical part is where I actually make mistakes. I am not sure if stopping solves anything or only removes the place where I can make mistakes.

I want to study electrical engineering, not because I have "golden hands", like my babcia says, but because I want to understand batteries well enough that I don't need to guess and say "it's fine."

The part I have not solved is 230 złoty. That is what I kept. His number is still in my phone as "Oleh Pro2 Glovo". I have opened BLIK, our phone payment app, about four times. I have the 230, that is not the problem. The problem is the message box. I would have to write something in it, and I don't know what. In June I saw him on Grunwaldzka on a new Ninebot, not from me. I didn't wave. He never left a review on OLX. I still check sometimes.
