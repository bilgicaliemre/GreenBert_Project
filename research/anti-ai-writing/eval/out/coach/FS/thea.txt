The biggest problem: your last sentence

"I think it might be the same thing I want to learn in statistics: how to count people without forgetting that every number was a person."

One paragraph earlier you wrote "I don't know if I like the math because it makes it bigger, like all the counties at once, or because it makes it smaller and easier," and "Sometimes I think the spreadsheet is so I don't have to feel 48 separate things." Those are the most honest lines in the essay, and they work because you don't know the answer. The last sentence answers it anyway: it turns your doubt into a goal, two sentences after you say you "still don't know exactly what she means." It is also the only sentence in the draft that could be pasted into someone else's statistics essay.

You don't need it to make the connection. Arlene's text says "BREATHE WITH THEM," and the whole essay is about counting people. Readers will put those two together on their own, and they'll believe it more because you didn't do it for them.
