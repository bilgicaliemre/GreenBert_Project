New cells would have eaten my margin, so I used salvaged ones, and with no 400-złoty spot welder I soldered the strips. Nineteen days later, at 22:10 on Grunwaldzka in the rain, it cut out with an order in his bag. He pushed it about four kilometers to my building.

When he came down to the bike room, I was thinking about his 380 złoty. Before unscrewing anything I said, "It got wet." I pointed at the water in the bottom of the pack (there's always some, it's Gdańsk) and gave him back 150. Two days later I found my cracked solder joint and the cell beside it, cooked. I've opened BLIK four times to send the other 230 and stopped each time at the message box.
