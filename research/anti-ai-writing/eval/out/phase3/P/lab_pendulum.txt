Discussion

Our results support the model T = 2π√(L/g). The graph of T² against L is a straight line with a slope of 4.105 s²/m and an intercept of about −0.002 s², which is effectively zero, so the line passes through the origin as predicted. From the slope, g = 4π²/4.105 = 9.62 m/s², about 1.9% below the accepted value of 9.81 m/s².

The error looks systematic rather than random. All five mean periods were about 1% longer than predicted. At 1.00 m, for example, the formula gives 2.006 s but we measured 2.026 s. Because g depends on T², a 1% error in the period becomes roughly a 2% error in g, which matches our result.

The most likely cause is the string. The proper string had run out, so we used cotton twine, and it stretched: the 1.00 m length measured about 1.01 m after a few minutes with the bob hanging. Using 1.01 m for that point gives g ≈ 9.71 m/s², closing about half the gap. The other lengths were not re-measured, so I cannot say how much each one stretched, but a real length longer than the recorded one would make every period too long, which is the pattern we saw.

Timing explains the pattern less well. My reaction time is about 0.2 s and the spread within each length was at most 0.15 s over 10 swings, but random errors like these would push some periods up and others down. Jonah thinks I started the stopwatch late a couple of times, which would make times shorter, not longer. The release angle may have added a little, since it was only estimated by eye below 0.60 m, and wider swings take slightly longer than the formula predicts.

We left out one trial. At 0.60 m, trial 2 (14.11 s) was almost certainly 9 swings, not 10, because Jonah lost count when Mr. Pell came in. Dividing by 9 gives 1.568 s, nearly identical to the 1.569 s mean of the other two trials. Including it lowers R² from 1.000 to 0.997 but barely changes the slope.

Next time we would use a less stretchy string and measure each length after the bob has hung for a few minutes, use the protractor at every length, and try Jonah's phone light-gate app to remove reaction-time and counting errors.
