Dear future roommate,

Fair warning: I count things. I'm the organist at Immanuel Lutheran in Hartington, Nebraska, and the funeral home calls me too. I've played 48 funerals since June 2024 and never been to one as just a guest. Duane, the funeral director, and I guess which hymn each family will pick; I'm up 19 to 14.

Junior year I charted my Immanuel tally (31 funerals, 6 baptisms) in Google Sheets with a trend line and posted it to the county Facebook group: Immanuel empty by 2045, Hartington by about 2070, "Just math!" Kathy Wieseler, who runs a daycare, commented "Come count my 22 kids, honey." I told her anecdotes aren't data. My whole post was an anecdote: one church. I deleted it after four hours. My mom, the rural mail carrier, made me write Kathy an apology, then delivered it with a real stamp so it was legal.

On the Census website, trying to prove I was right, I found demography. I want to study that, or statistics. The county probably is shrinking, just not by my math. But I think I wanted the town to be dying, because then leaving wouldn't really be leaving anybody. That chart was me making myself a permission slip with a trend line.

I still can't tell if I like the math because it makes things bigger, every county at once, or smaller and easier. Sometimes I think the spreadsheet is so I don't have to feel 48 separate things.

Thea
