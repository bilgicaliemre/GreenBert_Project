Dear roommate,

I play the organ in socks, because my shoes slip on the pedals. At a funeral lunch once, a little kid pointed at me and said, "That lady has cats on her feet." I'm the organist at Immanuel Lutheran in Hartington, Nebraska, and my brother calls me the Grim Reaper's DJ. I've played 48 funerals since June 2024 (I keep track).

Keeping track got me in trouble in February of junior year. I put my Immanuel count (31 funerals, 6 baptisms) into a Google Sheets chart and posted in the county Facebook group that Hartington would be empty by about 2070. "Just math!" Kathy Wieseler, who runs a daycare, commented, "Come count my 22 kids, honey." I told her anecdotes aren't data, when my whole post was one church. Before deleting it, I screenshotted her comment and sent it to the quiz bowl group chat to make fun of her. My mom, Kathy's mail carrier, made me write an apology and delivered it with a real stamp.

Nobody knew I liked the 63 comments, especially the mad ones. I think I wanted the town to be dying, because then leaving wouldn't be leaving anybody. The 2070 thing was me making myself a permission slip with a trend line.

If you catch me making a chart about something I'm about to leave, ask me what it's for. I still can't tell whether the spreadsheet makes 48 funerals bigger or just keeps me from feeling 48 separate things.

Thea
